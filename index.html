How to play

Reveal two cards. Matching symbols stay face-up; unmatched cards flip back. Match every pair to win!

  • Time Attack: Complete the board before time runs out.
  • Zen Mode: Play without a countdown.
  • Combos: Consecutive matches multiply your points.
  • Hints: Briefly reveal a matching pair at a score cost.
  • Keyboard: Use arrow keys to navigate cards, Enter or Space to flip, P to pause, H for a hint.
